## 1. Delivery policy

Phases reduce integration risk, not requirements. The first vertical slice proves core invariants with a small synthetic campaign. It is explicitly not the completed game. Every required system remains on the delivery path. Seeded acceptance scenarios arrange relevant circumstances but do not force those circumstances into ordinary campaigns.

No reliable calendar estimate or operating cost is asserted until world scale, fidelity and model budget are agreed and benchmarked. Full globe, detailed forces, years of history and independent AI actors make this a substantial simulation project. Progress is measured by exit tests and playable behavior, not screen count.

## 3. Acceptance scenarios

### A01 — Five-year energy and coercion (§§75, 78)

Arrange an explicitly synthetic 2026 world with uranium/lithium demand, supplier dependence, industrial processing capacity and Chinese strategic objectives. Confirm a nuclear/renewable/storage policy; record decision and causal demand effects. Later conclude an Australian agreement, build domestic refining and allow production/recycling capacity to become operational at their actual completion dates. Advance years with unrelated events and conversations retained in the archive.

Trigger an independently meaningful diplomatic deterioration; have China evaluate available economic coercion. The context manifest must contain the old policy, current demand, Australian agreement, actual refining throughput, relevant threads and actor knowledge. It must not contain the entire transcript. Record a reasoned abandonment of the mineral embargo when capacity and substitution undermine leverage. A later collected report can reveal it; no immediate omniscient Home alert is permitted.

Assertions: original records survive, mitigating edges exist, capacity is quantitatively sufficient under the configured rule, strategy is abandoned before execution, and no coercive losses occur from a canceled plan. Counterfactual fixture without mitigation must yield greater measured vulnerability and a meaningfully different planning evaluation, without forcing war or a single mandatory action. Sweep delay/failure of the refinery to demonstrate dates matter. Grow irrelevant transcript volume and verify bounded model input and continued causal retrieval.

### A02 — Naval expansion and politics (§76)

Arrange insufficient treasury room without borrowing and insufficient initial legislative support. Generate a COA/program recommendation, finance assessment and observer vote estimates. Player meets leaders, modifies the program, negotiates concessions, addresses the public and pressures senators. Each interaction has a structured proposal and consequence appropriate to its authorization; mere hypothetical chat does not execute policy.

For the success fixture, configured preferences and seeded outcomes lead to narrow passage; a second fixture allows failure. Assertions: support shifts have sources; true private positions remain hidden; projected vote and final result can diverge; procedure/quorum applies; appropriation enables commitments; borrowing changes debt; industry changes production schedule; ships arrive only after construction; long-term political and readiness effects link back to decisions. A blocked appropriation cannot be bypassed by asking a model to purchase ships directly.

### A03 — Independent hurricane (§77)

Create a scheduled or stochastic hurricane in Florida while the player's immediate agenda concerns another region. Process landfall during an authorized turn. Collection yields estimated casualties/outages/damage and geographic evidence. Map marker opens a player-safe event profile. Assertions: marker/fields/confidence reflect observation, underlying true impacts propagate to relevant infrastructure/economy/politics, and an unaffected player nation need not receive forced strategic consequences. Event generation does not depend on the player requesting a disaster story.

## 5. Seed data and evaluation discipline

Fixtures are synthetic and labeled, with source provenance and no claim of current real-world accuracy. Build a small IRR scenario containing a configurable Emperor/Pro Consul/Senate, distinct ministers, a few foreign actors including China/Australia/United States, energy resources, legislative procedure, representative units, stocks and Florida geometry. Include Michael Bennett as a deliberate alternate-history override. The only fixed inventory number demanded by the source example is the interceptor test quantity; other invented fixture numbers are marked test assumptions.

A second fixture uses a different nation, offices and government procedure to prove no IRR hard-coding. Production creation supports broad world data and detailed forces; fixture size is not the production actor cap. Each fixture has a versioned manifest of entities, relationships, starting quantities, private beliefs, rules, random seed and expected invariant outcomes.

Model evaluation separates mechanical correctness from qualitative plausibility. Mechanical assertions must be reproducible; actor dialogue evaluation checks consistency with doctrine/information, meaningful disagreement, willingness to refuse, no railroading and evidence quality. Statistical sweeps assess stability/sensitivity of political and military outcomes; no single convincing narrative validates calibration.

## 6. Performance, cost and operational gates

Proposed benchmark profiles (not approved product limits): a compact integration world, a medium multi-nation world and a world using the accepted Q06 major/minor relevance tiers. For each, run a week/month/six-month request, many dormant threads, concurrent account sessions and five simulated years of history. Record p50/p95 turn completion, largest checkpoint CPU/memory, DB query time, retrieval tokens, model spend, total stored history and recovery time.

Budgets remain configurable and visible; reaching a cap pauses work rather than discarding required agents/systems. Large intervals may need multiple workflow instances with stable checkpoint continuation. Verify platform limits against the selected account and versions before release. Measure bounded memory independently of campaign transcript size.

Release gate: documented backup/restore rehearsal; migrations from clean and prior schema versions; secrets/access audit; no campaign leakage; operational alerts for stuck turns; observer-safe logs; defined cost envelope; source acceptance scenarios together; actual iPhone test. User decides whether remaining product questions or known defects permit release.
